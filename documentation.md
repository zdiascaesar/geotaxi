### Support System
Located in `lib/support_chat/` and `lib/side_bar_client/`

The support system provides comprehensive customer assistance through multiple channels.

#### Support Page
Main hub for customer support featuring:
1. **FAQ Section**
   - Categorized help topics:
     - Account Management
     - Payment & Billing
     - Help & Support
   - Interactive Q&A format
   - Detailed answers for common issues
   - Easy navigation with categories

2. **Previous Requests**
   - History of support interactions
   - Status tracking
   - Request details and responses
   - Historical context preservation

3. **Contact Information**
   - Support team contact details
   - Emergency contact numbers
   - Office locations and hours
   - Communication channels

4. **Direct Support Access**
   - Live chat initiation
   - Support ticket creation
   - Priority routing system
   - Real-time assistance
